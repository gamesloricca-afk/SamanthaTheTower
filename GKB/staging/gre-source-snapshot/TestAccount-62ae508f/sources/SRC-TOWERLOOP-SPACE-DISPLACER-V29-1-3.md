## What the page states

- Space Displacer can replace Workshop Land Mines with moving Inner Land Mines.
- Replacement chance is 15% Epic, 20% Legendary, 25% Mythic, and 30% Ancestral.
- The converted-mine limit is 20.
- The page distinguishes Workshop Land Mine Chance from Space Displacer's conversion chance.
- It explicitly says ordinary ILM / ILM+ advice does not automatically apply to Workshop mines, and vice versa.
